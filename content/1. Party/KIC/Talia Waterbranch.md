# Appearance
Fair skin, yet freckled and sun kissed (damaged lmfao). Big mane of curly, red hair sometimes kept in a bun or ponytail. Mid 20s. Often wears a green chemise and dark pantaloons, a dark navy blue cloak for poor weather.

# Backstory
Her family is among a [[The Travelling Troupe|group of travellers]] who believe in living slowly and living quietly. Run ins with unsavoury folk has pushed her into a position of defence with her group – she certainly knows her way around a sword. She couldn’t see herself growing as a person as long as she stuck with the community, so she set off on her own to see the world. Her naivete caused some friction between her and the local law as she would sometimes say questionable things about nobility or commit outright theft. She continues to travel after 3 years, laying low but lending an eager helping hand to whoever will accept it.

# Personality
Aloof, friendly, but knowledgeable of the ways of the world. Regarding herself a bit of a ‘Robin Hood’ – and regarded by some as a criminal – Talia is a traveller who gives and takes what she can, even if it isn’t hers to give or take. She’s an avid enjoyer of arts and culture and finds herself happiest when in good company.
